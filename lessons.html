Lesson 1: Basic Introduction to Spanish

Spanish is one of the most widely spoken languages, with over 500 million native speakers! It comes from Latin and is part of the Romance language family, along with French, Italian, and Portuguese. In this lesson, you'll learn what makes Spanish unique, from its alphabet to its verb conjugations. ¡Vamos!

Lesson 2: Conjugations

Spanish verbs change their endings based on who's doing the action and when it's happening — this is called conjugation. There are three main verb types: -ar, -er, and -ir, each with their own patterns. Once you learn the endings, you can start forming real sentences!

Lesson 3: The Basic Structure of Sentences

Spanish usually follows a subject-verb-object order, but it's flexible! You can often drop the subject if the verb makes it clear who's acting. Mastering these patterns helps you sound more natural and expressive.

Lesson 4: Spanish Pronunciation

Spanish pronunciation is refreshingly consistent — words are pronounced just as they're written! In this lesson, you'll discover how Spanish vowels always keep the same sound, how to handle the "ñ," and why word stress matters.

Lesson 16: Imperfect Past vs. Simple Past

Spanish has two main ways to talk about the past: the preterite and the imperfect. The preterite is for finished actions. The imperfect is for things that used to happen or were ongoing. Mastering this difference will make your stories flow naturally!
